<Phrase chinese="我向你学习。" pinyin="wǒ xiàng nǐ xué xí." gloss="I learn from you." />

向你 means “from you” in this sentence. The person need not be a professional teacher.

<ClassicQuote source="main" />

Walking with others, I am sure to find a teacher among them. 三人行 is the opening image of this saying, still used to evoke learning from those around us.

The passage goes on to distinguish choosing what is good from noticing faults and correcting ourselves. Learning from someone does not mean copying everything they do.

What could you learn from someone you met today?
