<Phrase chinese="我想了解自己。" pinyin="wǒ xiǎng liǎo jiě zì jǐ." gloss="I want to understand myself." />

我想 means “I want to”; 了解自己 means “understand myself”. This is an everyday way into the more reflective expression 自知.

<ClassicQuote source="main" />

Knowing others is wisdom; knowing yourself is clarity. The Daodejing puts these two kinds of understanding side by side, turning attention toward our own motives and limits.

What is one habit you notice more easily in others than in yourself?
