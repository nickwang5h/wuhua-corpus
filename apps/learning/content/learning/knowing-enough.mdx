<Phrase chinese="我很知足。" pinyin="wǒ hěn zhī zú." gloss="I'm content." />

知 means “know”; 足 means “enough” here. In the modern sentence, 我 means “I”.

<ClassicQuote source="main" />

Someone who knows what is enough is rich. The Daodejing treats contentment as a kind of wealth, rather than measuring wealth only by possessions. This is a way to reflect on our desires, not a reason to tell others to accept deprivation.

Why might knowing “enough” change what it means to be rich?
