我在学习 means “I am learning”; 中文 means “Chinese”. 学习 is the everyday modern word. The classic brings learning and practice together:

<ClassicQuote source="main" />

Isn't it a pleasure to learn and keep practising what you have learned? The Analects opens with joy in returning to learning, not simply collecting new information.

A sentence may become familiar through use; an idea may become clearer when you revisit it. What would you like to return to?
